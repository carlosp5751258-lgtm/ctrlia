Frequently asked questions

Is it really free?

Yes, all of it. There are no paid versions. The files for every piece are on GitHub under the MIT license, with no sign-up or email.

What will I find here?

Recipes (a problem solved step by step), references, comparisons, copy-ready templates and checklists. Filter by format or topic in the library.

Does it work on Windows?

Yes, with WSL or Git Bash. The Bash scripts are designed for Linux and macOS.

Does this site store my data?

Without an account, no. What you tick in the checklists and the light/dark theme stay only in your browser. No cookies or trackers.

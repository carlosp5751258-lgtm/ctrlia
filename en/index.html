Frequently asked questions

Is it really free?

Yes. All 10 tools are on GitHub under the MIT license. No sign-up or email required.

How do I install it?

Open the tool and follow the steps: it is almost always cloning the repository and copying one or two files into your project.

What is the Pro version?

The full pack for each tool (more templates, more workflows, the PDF guide…). It is optional: the free version works on its own. It is sold on Gumroad and you pay what you want from 2 or 3 USD.

Does it work on Windows?

Yes, with WSL or Git Bash. The Bash scripts are designed for Linux and macOS.

Does this site store my data?

No. It has no forms, cookies or trackers.
